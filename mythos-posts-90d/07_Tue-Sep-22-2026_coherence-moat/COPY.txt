Features get copied in a quarter. Coherence can't be copied at all.

Every moat you were taught is draining. Features ship everywhere within months. Distribution is rentable. Even network effects wobble when switching gets cheap.

The one thing that can't be cloned is accumulated coherence — years of story, decisions, and trust that all point the same direction. A competitor can copy what you built. They can't copy the record of you building it.

That record is an asset. Almost nobody treats it like one. We're building the vault for it.

The best moat was never in the product. It's in the provenance.

What part of your history would be hardest for a competitor to fake?

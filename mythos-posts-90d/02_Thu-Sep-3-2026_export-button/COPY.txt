The export button is the most honest feature in software.

You can learn everything about a company from how it treats your leaving. Buried the export three menus deep? They're betting on your inertia. No export at all? You were never the customer — you were the inventory.

We build the door out first, before the features. Not because we expect people to leave — because a yes only means something when leaving is easy.

Lock-in is a confession that the product can't keep you on its own.

Check your most important tool right now: could you leave with everything?

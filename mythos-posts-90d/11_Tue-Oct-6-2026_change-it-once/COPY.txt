Change the number once. It changes everywhere. That's the whole trick.

Last month a partner corrected one revenue assumption. In a normal company that's a week of archaeology — the deck, the one-pager, the model, the site, all disagreeing about the same number.

Here it was one edit. The documents all render from a single source of truth, so the correction propagated in minutes, and every surface told the same story at dinner that the model told at lunch.

Boring? Completely. But investors don't lose trust over bad numbers. They lose trust over numbers that disagree with each other.

Inconsistency reads as dishonesty, even when it's just entropy.

How many places would you have to edit to change one number about your business?

Your values are data. Nobody's system treats them that way.

Your calendar is structured. Your invoices are structured. The things you'd never compromise on? Those live in your head, invisible to every tool making suggestions all day long.

So software optimizes what it can see — clicks, engagement, output — and quietly steamrolls what it can't. Not out of malice. The values were never in the system.

We made them first-class citizens: stored, versioned, consulted. When the work drifts from the values, the substrate notices before you do.

What gets encoded gets protected. What stays implicit gets optimized away.

What value of yours has software quietly steamrolled?

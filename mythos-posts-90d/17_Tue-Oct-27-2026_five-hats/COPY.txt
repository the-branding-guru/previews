Monday I'm a game publisher. Tuesday, a health network. Wednesday, a record label. I'm always me.

I wrote before about context-switching being the thing that breaks multi-venture founders. Two months later, here's the practice report: the switching cost didn't disappear. It moved.

It moved out of my head and into the substrate. Each venture's world — its voice, its state, its open threads — is held outside me. I don't reload it from memory at 6am. I walk in and it's already there, current, waiting.

The energy that used to go into remembering who I am today goes into building instead.

You can wear five hats. You just can't afford five heads.

What does switching contexts actually cost you, per day?

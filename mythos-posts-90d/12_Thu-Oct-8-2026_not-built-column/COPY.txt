Our capability map has a "not built" column. On purpose.

Before a big meeting, we publish a document called What Is Actually Built. Three columns: live, built-but-not-deployed, not built. The third column is the one people remember.

Everyone in tech has sat through the demo where everything "works." Everyone has later learned what "works" meant. The inflated demo is so universal that honesty reads as a plot twist.

Here's the thing: the not-built column doesn't cost you the room. It buys the other two columns their credibility.

Claim everything and they'll verify everything. Declare the gaps and they'll trust the rest.

Would your company survive publishing its own not-built column?

Personal brand is the costume. Identity is the spine.

"Personal brand" became an industry that teaches you to perform a consistent character. Color palette, content pillars, posting cadence. A costume, fitted well.

Costumes work until the wind changes. Then you're renegotiating who you are in public, and the audience can smell it.

Identity is underneath: the values that don't move, the story that explains every choice you've made. Get that layer solid and the brand on top stops being a performance — it's just you, dressed for the room.

We build for the spine, not the costume.

Which one have you been maintaining?

section 01 · The Parties
Who is who, and what each brings

Not a vendor. Not a client. A flagship.

Wonderstorm Academy — David Yarbrough and Holly Yarbrough — brings the school, the method, and the proof: the first school in the country with O.M.G. and social-skills intelligence woven into daily operations. StarHub Studios PBC brings the platform, three portals, growth operations, and the storytelling engine — already built, already delivered, carried at our risk. Neither side is buying from the other. One side brings a working school; the other brings the system that lets it travel.

◆ The discipline underneath every clause

Revenue you already have ⊥ revenue we create together ⊥ revenue from new digital lines ⊥ revenue from other schools. Four ledgers, kept apart, each with its own split. We never take a share of a dollar we had nothing to do with, and we never take a share large enough to make a child unprofitable to enroll. Where something is left open in this document, it is open on purpose, and it says who closes it.

section 02 · What This Is, and What It Is Not
Three things it could have been — and the one it is

A revenue partnership. Not an investment, not a sale.

What it is NOT Why that matters to you
Not an equity deal. StarHub takes no ownership interest in Wonderstorm Academy — none at formation, none on recovery, none ever, absent a separate agreement you would have to sign. Your cap table is untouched. Nothing here dilutes you, and nothing here gives us a vote in your school.
Not a licence of your method. The WonderStorm method, curriculum, brand and community are yours. We operate a platform; we do not acquire what runs on it. If this partnership ends, the method leaves with you — intact and unencumbered.
Not an invoice. There is no bill, no retainer due on a date, and no check to write for the build. If the system never generates a dollar, you never owe one. That is the whole shape of the deal.

What it IS: a defined commercial partnership in which StarHub carries the cost of a platform it has already delivered, and recovers that cost only out of revenue the platform itself creates — then shares in continued growth for as long as it is actively producing it.

section 04 · The Commercial Terms
Every dollar, and whose it is

Recovery is capped. Then, and only then, we share.

The platform's conservative market replacement value exceeds $250,000. Wonderstorm's founding-partner principal is fixed at $50,000 — a deep founding discount, and a number that does not move regardless of how long recovery takes. Growth operations carry a market equivalent of $19,000–$40,000 per month and are provided at our risk.

◆ The rule we will not break

We will not name a percentage of your tuition until we know what a seat actually earns you. A share above your margin would mean every child we bring costs you money — and we will not build that. Recovery is capped at one-third of contribution margin for exactly this reason, and the completion receipt is visible the day it clears.

section 06 · How This Protects You
The exits, the caps, the receipts

Every commitment here has a way out.

Protection What it means in practice
The 60-day out-clause Sixty days in, if it is not earning its keep, we part friends and the site stays yours. No penalty, no clawback, no argument.
The recovery cap Never more than one-third of contribution margin on enrollments the system created. At any margin. A child the platform brings can never cost you money to enroll.
The fixed principal $50,000, and it does not grow. No interest, no escalation, no fees added during recovery. The monthly report projects the clearing date; a completion receipt issues the day it clears.
The activity condition The 20% post-recovery share applies only while StarHub is actively running growth operations. We stop working, we stop sharing.
Attribution is measured, not asserted "StarHub-attributed" means instrumented end-to-end and visible in the monthly report. If we cannot show it in the numbers, we do not claim it. The attribution definition is OPEN — §07 slot 4.
Your data stays yours Family and student data is processed on your instruction, never sold, never used to train anything, and returned or destroyed at exit.

The game plan.

What you told me on the call, what I heard underneath it, and what gets built so the right people find you.

What you said

The one thing: "Oh yeah, now people will find me. The right people."

The frequency you want: corporate results with the spiritual side finally in the open. Authentic you, so they know what they're getting. Playful, powerful, loving.

The obstacle: visibility. Two interviews cost you time and returned nothing. Word of mouth brings people back, but nothing goes out to find new ones.

The mission: co-creating heaven on earth. A legacy that lives in each client, not in you having to do it.

What I heard underneath

The frequency nowAligned, big time. The work is real, the results are documented, the story is extraordinary. But the site sells the corporate half in 2017 clothes, and the price lives in your head, so every conversation renegotiates it.
The frequency you need to winThe generosity is the brand and the leak at the same time. So the brand does the pricing for you. The site says the number before you have to. Free becomes one designed door, not an open one.

What the skeleton already does

  1. Leads with both halves. "You did the proving. Now comes the giving." Corporate results, spiritual roots, in the first breath.
  2. Tells the 1977 story on the home page, not buried under About. It is the reason people trust you; it belongs at the front.
  3. Puts your prices on the page. Three lengths, your real numbers, paid-in-full noted. The visitor decides calmly; you never discount on a call again.
  4. Names the free door. One 20-minute call. Not a free session. The path from free to paid is visible.
  5. Uses only what is yours. Your logo, your photograph, your testimonials, your roadmap, your words. Nothing invented.
  6. Works on a phone. Every section, every width.

How we work

Structure: a joint venture, as you chose on the call. The terms are Jack's to walk you through in person, not to print here.

Rhythm: one call a week, forty-five minutes, site business only. You receive an updated project plan after every call: what was accomplished, what's outstanding from you, next steps. Jack presents every review on screen share; you never review alone.

Your homework, total: the brochure draft, the Bluehost login path, the writing from 1977, and the names of the people who send you clients unasked.

The game plan.

What you told me on the call, what I heard underneath it, and what gets built so the right people find you.

What you said

The one thing: "Oh yeah, now people will find me. The right people."

The frequency you want: corporate results with the spiritual side finally in the open. Authentic you, so they know what they're getting. Playful, powerful, loving.

The obstacle: visibility. Two interviews cost you time and returned nothing. Word of mouth brings people back; nothing goes out to find new ones.

The mission: co-creating heaven on earth. A legacy that lives in each client, not in you having to do it.

What I heard underneath

The frequency nowAligned, big time. The work is real, the results are documented, the story is extraordinary. But the site sells the corporate half in 2017 clothes, and the price lives in your head, so every conversation renegotiates it.
The frequency you need to winThe generosity is the brand and the leak at the same time. So the brand does the pricing for you. The site says the number before you have to. Free becomes one designed door, not an open one.

What this skeleton already does

  1. Leads with both halves. "You did the proving. Now comes the giving." Corporate results, spiritual roots, in the first breath.
  2. Tells the 1977 story on its own page, then the thirty years that followed. It is the reason people trust you.
  3. Puts your prices on the page, with a paid-in-full switch. The visitor decides calmly; you never discount on a call again.
  4. Names the free door. One 20-minute call, booked in two taps. Not a free session.
  5. Makes the visitor participate. Tap the checklist, walk the roadmap, flip the paradigm. A page that responds is a page people finish.
  6. Uses only what is yours. Your logo, your photograph, your testimonials, your roadmap, your words. Nothing invented.
  7. Holds on every screen. Phone, tablet, laptop, wide.

The new paradigm

Two ways to lead. Only one of them lasts.

You have been successful. There is an impulse inside calling for more: to share what you know in a world hungry for wholeness. Tap either side.

The old paradigm

  • Proving. Gaining knowledge and experience.
  • Having. Competition to be the best.
  • Acquiring. Conflict = win-lose.
  • "I" success. Focus on self.
  • Problems solved with stress and force in the face of chaos.

The new paradigm

  • Giving and receiving = sustainability. Sharing wisdom.
  • Contributing. Co-creating shared success.
  • Generosity and abundance. Creative conflict = win-win-win.
  • "We" success. Inspiring others to action.
  • Opportunities created from problems, with creativity, fun and calm in the face of chaos.

Is this you?

The new paradigm

Two ways to lead. Only one of them lasts.

You have been successful. There is an impulse inside calling for more: to share what you know in a world hungry for wholeness.

The old paradigm

  • Proving. Gaining knowledge and experience.
  • Having. Competition to be the best.
  • Acquiring. Conflict = win-lose.
  • "I" success. Focus on self.
  • Problems solved with stress and force in the face of chaos.

The new paradigm

  • Giving and receiving = sustainability. Sharing wisdom.
  • Contributing. Co-creating shared success.
  • Generosity and abundance. Creative conflict = win-win-win.
  • "We" success. Inspiring others to action.
  • Opportunities created from problems, with creativity, fun and calm in the face of chaos.

The story

1977. The night everything changed.

My stepfather died suddenly at 47. Three weeks later, two viruses, five days of 105° fever, and I was on my living room floor, riddled with fear that I was going to die alone.

Then I was guided to bring white light into my body. I became connected to the entire universe, one with everyone and everything. That day I knew, clearly, that there is a gift in everything that happens.

I have spent every year since bringing that truth into boardrooms. Fifteen years as an executive coach. Co-creator of Northrop Grumman's internal coaching program and one of its Master Coaches. Six coaching centers, 24,000 employees, participation up 65% in three years.

Playful. Powerful. Loving. That is how I coach, and it is what comes out in you.

section 01 · Frequency
6 questions

Frequency

# Ask it like this What you're listening for
1 Tell me about the brand in one breath — as if I were a stranger at a bar.
2 What frequency are you on right now? How does the brand feel today, to you, on an honest day?
3 What frequency do you want to be on? Who do you want to be when someone finds you?
4 What frequency do you NEED to be on to win?
Your read, said aloud. The gap between 2 and 4 is the engagement.
5 Who do you admire in your space, and what exactly do they have that you don't?
6 When someone leaves a conversation with you, what do you want them saying?

Mission & Purpose

# Ask it like this What you're listening for
7 What is the mission of the brand? One sentence.
8 Why do YOU carry this mission? What happened that made this yours?
9 What would be lost if this brand did not exist?
10 What are you not willing to do, even for money?
11 Where do you want this in three years — and is that a lifestyle, a company, or a legacy?

Obstacles

# Ask it like this What you're listening for
12 What is the single biggest thing standing between you and where you want to be?
13 What have you already tried that did not work? What did it cost you?
14 Who has told you no, and what reason did they give?
15 What do you believe is broken that you cannot fix yourself?
16 What are you afraid I will find when I look at your brand?

section 04 · The Close
5 questions

The Close

# Ask it like this What you're listening for
27 "Within 24 to 72 hours I'll bring you the skeleton of your brand — the outline, the game plan, and enough built that you can click it. Then we get on a call and I walk you through it."
Say it. Tick when said.
28 Which are we doing — work for hire, or a joint venture?
Ask on this call. Their answer tells you how much they believe in their own thing.
29 Name the three payment routes: paid in full up front (best price) · deposit plus payments · deferred against the revenue the work generates.
So cost never becomes the reason they stall.
30 Confirm the call: day, time, who else is on it.
31 What is the ONE thing that, if I showed it to you, would make you say yes?
Build that first. It is the skeleton's spine.
